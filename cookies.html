Cookie Policy

This portfolio primarily uses browser local storage rather than tracking cookies.

Essential storage

The desktop uses local storage for wallpaper choice, first-visit state, app frequency, lock state, and consent preference. These features do not require optional analytics consent.

Optional analytics

Vercel Analytics, Speed Insights, and anonymous Supabase event logging are loaded only after you select Allow analytics.

Managing choices

Use the Privacy Policy settings link to review your decision. You can also clear site data in your browser settings.
