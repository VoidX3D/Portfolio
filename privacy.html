Privacy Policy

VoidX3D keeps this portfolio simple and privacy-minded. Essential preferences stay in your browser. Optional analytics are disabled until you choose to allow them.

What is collected

If you allow analytics, the site may record an anonymous session identifier, page or app events, locale, and timezone. The site does not intentionally store raw IP addresses or sensitive personal details in analytics. Contact messages include the information you submit.

Why it is collected

Analytics help identify broken apps and improve the interface. Contact details are used only to respond to your message.

Your choices

You can reject optional analytics, change your choice, or clear local storage from your browser. Functional preferences such as wallpaper and app history can be reset from the desktop context menu.

Retention and sharing

Analytics and contact records are stored in the VoidX3D Supabase project. Contact submissions are private and are not published. Service providers may process data only to operate hosting, analytics, email, and database features.

Contact
